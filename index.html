How SlideFit changes a PPTX template

PowerPoint stores a deck as a zip of XML. The words, pictures, and tables live on slides. The look lives on the slide master, the layouts, and the theme. SlideFit keeps the template’s master and writes your content into the closest layout.

  1. Read the deck Titles, subtitles, bullets, pictures, and tables are pulled out of each slide. Charts and SmartArt are left behind, and the page says so.
  2. Pick a layout An opening title maps to Title Slide. A short divider maps to Section Header. Bullets map to Title and Content. A picture with text maps to Picture with Caption.
  3. Write a new file Your originals are not edited. The download is a new .pptx that opens in PowerPoint or LibreOffice, with the template’s fonts and colours.

Questions

Does the deck leave my computer?

No. JSZip opens the file in the page. There is no account and no SlideFit server. If you pay, PayPal sees the payment, not the slides. Read the privacy note.

Will it look exactly like I designed it?

It will look like the template. Content is placed in placeholders, so a custom collage will become a title, a text block, and pictures. That is the point of applying a company template.

What is the free limit?

Five content slides, then a SlideFit watermark slide. A longer deck is cut off at five until you unlock it. The samples include seven slides so you can see the cutoff.

Which template file should I use?

Any .pptx that already uses your master — a blank company deck is ideal. A .potx works too. SlideFit drops the template’s content slides and keeps the master, layouts, and theme.
